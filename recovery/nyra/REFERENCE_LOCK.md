- Mature adult semi-realistic face, natural eye scale, feminine facial planes, rose lips, dark eyes/brows and warm skin. Preserve the large official portrait's identity, not a generic “beautiful woman.”
- Dark high wavy ponytail, purple highlight/accent, swept face-framing locks.
- Fitted black/purple crop top, visible midriff; purple cropped jacket dropped off the shoulders, white sleeve inserts and rib trim.
- Black fitted leggings with sweeping purple panel accents, black belt, compact purple/dark utility accessories and fingerless gloves.
- Purple/black/white athletic sneakers, restrained silver hoops and purple pendant.
- Adult athletic feminine proportions and the master image's silhouette; no return to oversized Rain eyes/head or coral clothing.

Front and rear: same neutral near-A stance, parallel feet, full hair/hands/shoes, plain pale background. Side: strict left profile for body depth and garment placement. ## Observed limitations / items to approve

- Global palette, outfit categories, off-shoulder construction, mature proportions, hair accent and sneaker direction are consistent with the master.
- Derived images are not calibrated orthographic measurements. Front/rear scale and cutout baselines must be aligned during input preparation. Do not claim a mathematically consistent multi-view reconstruction.
- Small emblems, buckles, zipper paths, sneaker paneling and exact purple seam paths are regenerated interpretations. Their details are not approved replacements for the board. Redraw them deliberately during modeling/texturing.
- Larger pouch is assigned to the character's right hip and smaller case to the left, using the master front as primary. Cross-panel accessory details on the board are not completely specified; this interpretation requires review.
- Profile and rear ponytail-tip shape/length and hidden garment folds vary slightly. Use the master's rear silhouette to resolve them; do not average incompatible detail into geometry.
- Hair obscures part of the rear top. Hidden construction is an inference, not established reference data.
- Backgrounds are opaque, not alpha mattes. Mask inspection is required before generation so backdrop geometry and damaged thin parts are not introduced.
