# NYRA Candidate A reconstruction

**Review result: substantially improved construction and readability. The semi-realistic facial identity and premium finish remain below the approved concept.** The rebuilt asset stops at independent visual review.

The working master opens the locked Candidate A Blender source. It preserves the original mesh and two 4096 × 4096 atlases inside a hidden archive collection. Candidate A's presentation frame, stance and body landmarks guide the reconstruction. The leggings also use sampled depth from its actual surface. The head, skin trunk, jacket, hair, gloves, equipment and shoes required localized replacement geometry because their intended surfaces were fused or malformed.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Topology and materials

The audit's 448,590 raw flags correspond to **one-face boundary edges** across 1,661,862 edges in the GLB import, about 27.0% of edges. The audit's reported 46.8% uses a triangle denominator. On a temporary copy, a positional weld at a tolerance of 0.5 µm in source coordinates revealed 745 boundary edges and 5,189 edges with more than two incident faces. UV and normal splits contribute to the raw count. These measurements do not establish which visual pieces should be deleted.

The reconstructed working surfaces replaced the raw debris and fused regions. The original archive remains intact. The torso, filled shoe uppers and soles, ponytail core, and evaluated garment shells have closed surfaces in the targeted topology check. The face's eye openings and the pant hems and waistband are intentional. Hair cards, panel overlays, and jewelry have construction-specific boundaries. The fitted leggings and torso have UV layouts, with the latter generated by Blender's Smart UV Project. The visible review assembly measures 929,364 evaluated triangles across 564 editable objects, including converted bevel curves and excluding the hidden source and studio. The master is a high resolution review asset. A reconciled production UV layout, runtime triangle reduction and deformation topology are separate work.

The master packs a baked face albedo, procedural brown-eye pigment, a strand alpha texture and a sampled tangent-space knit normal tile. These four files are also in `textures/`. The face shader keeps editable color attributes active with the bake available in the node graph. Material collections distinguish skin, moist eye surfaces, lips, hair, fabric, leather, hardware and rubber. Subsurface scattering and some pore and hair detail are Blender look development. Real-time shader equivalence has not been measured.
